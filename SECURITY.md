# Security Policy

Please report vulnerabilities privately through the
[Cadence OSS security advisory form](https://github.com/AzzCraft/cadence-oss/security/advisories/new).
Do not publish exploit details before a fix or mitigation is available. We aim
to acknowledge reports within five business days and provide a status update
within thirty days.

Supported public version: `1.0.x` after its release tag is published.
